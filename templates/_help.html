How to review a line

  • AI Translated is the machine translation of the English line — a first draft, not a final answer.
  • Reviewer's Edit is pre-filled with that AI Translated text. Proofread it and correct it in this box — this is the only box you edit, and it's what gets narrated.
  • Regenerate audio makes the AI voice read your Reviewer's Edit text again — use it any time you change the wording.
  • 🎤 Record / Upload audio — if the AI narration mispronounces something or the delivery sounds off, record (or upload) your own reading to give it a corrected performance.
  • 💬 — click it next to a box to add a general comment, or first highlight a specific word or phrase in the text to comment on just that part (Confluence comments).
